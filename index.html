COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A few of the things customers ask before starting a concrete project.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Eastland also provides free planning, options and ideas to help you think through the project.

Do you do concrete sealing?

Yes. Sealing can help protect concrete from moisture, weather exposure, UV rays, oil, salt and other everyday elements.

Will you handle tear-out of existing concrete?

Yes. Eastland can handle the tear-out and removal of concrete that needs to be replaced.

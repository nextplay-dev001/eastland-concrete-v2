ABOUT EASTLAND

Family owned. Built on referrals.

Eastland Concrete works across decorative, residential and light commercial concrete projects with a focus on quality work and customer satisfaction from start to finish.

Concrete work without the runaround.

The way Eastland describes its work is simple: provide professional expertise, quality service, fair estimates and upfront pricing. That straightforward approach has helped the business grow largely through customer referrals.

What customers can expect

  • Free estimates with planning, options and ideas
  • Residential, decorative and light commercial concrete work
  • Tear-out and removal when concrete needs to be replaced
  • Concrete sealing to help protect finished surfaces
  • Direct communication throughout the project

Made to last

From a driveway or patio to a more custom concrete project, the goal is to leave the customer with work that looks right, functions well and holds up over time.
